Our story

A quiet place to pause

Phintopia began with a dream of a calm, welcoming place away from the noise of everyday life. It's inspired by traditional Vietnamese phin coffee, where a slow drip turns each cup into a small ritual.

Alongside slow-brewed coffee we serve matcha, herbal teas and seasonal favourites, made for moments of comfort and renewal.
